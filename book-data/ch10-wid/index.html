Pakistan top income shares data pack

Public Economics for Developing Countries, Chapter 10, Optimal Taxation, Data problem 17.

One plain CSV file cut from the World Inequality Database (WID.world), Pakistan file WID_data_PK.csv (bulk download, version of 13 November 2026): the share of pre-tax national income (variable sptinc992j: adults, income split equally within couples) held by the top 1 percent (p99p100) and the top 0.1 percent (p99.9p100), for 1990 to 2025, with WID's own data-quality score for each year. WID.world states that its data are licensed under a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License. Cite the database, not this pack: World Inequality Database, WID.world.
