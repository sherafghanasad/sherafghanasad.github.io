Pakistan top income shares, 1990-2025 (Chapter 10, Data problem 17)
Public Economics for Developing Countries

Source: World Inequality Database (WID.world), Pakistan file WID_data_PK.csv
from the bulk download (file dated 13 November 2026), cut on 19 November 2026.
WID.world states that its data are licensed under a Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International License. Cite: World Inequality Database,
WID.world. Full source: https://wid.world/country/pakistan/

File: wid-pakistan-top-shares.csv (comma-separated, one row per year)

  year          calendar year, 1990 to 2025
  top1_share    share of pre-tax national income held by the top 1 percent
                of adults (WID variable sptinc992j, percentile p99p100),
                as a fraction: 0.1665 means 16.65 percent
  top01_share   the same for the top 0.1 percent (percentile p99.9p100)
  data_quality  WID's data-quality score for that year, as it appears in
                the database file

Nothing else has been changed: values are WID's, rounded to six decimals.
