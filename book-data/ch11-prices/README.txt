Chapter 11 price-index data pack
Public Economics for Developing Countries, Chapter 11, Who Bears a Tax, Data problems 19 and 20.
Both files are unaltered copies of official statistics, downloaded on 26 November 2026. Cite the source, not this pack.

france-hicp-restaurants-hairdressing.csv
  Source: Eurostat, Harmonised Index of Consumer Prices, monthly data (index), dataset prc_hicp_midx.
  France, unit I15 (2015 = 100), monthly, from January 1996 to December 2025.
  Columns: DATAFLOW, LAST UPDATE, freq, unit, coicop, geo, TIME_PERIOD (the month), OBS_VALUE (the index), OBS_FLAG, CONF_STATUS.
  coicop CP1111 = restaurants, cafes and the like; CP1211 = hairdressing salons and personal grooming establishments.
  Eurostat froze this dataset at December 2025 when it moved to a new classification.
  Reuse: Eurostat authorises reuse of its data provided the source is acknowledged.

finland-cpi-hairdressing.csv
  Source: Statistics Finland, Consumer Price Index (2005 = 100), monthly data, archive table 11xf.
  Finland, monthly, from January 2005 (2005M01) to December 2025 (2025M12).
  Columns: Month, Commodity, Point figure (the index).
  Commodities: 12111 Hairdressing for men and children; 12112 Hairdressing for women; 12113 Personal grooming treatments.
  Reuse: Statistics Finland's open data are covered by the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence.
