Price-index data pack

Public Economics for Developing Countries, Chapter 11, Who Bears a Tax, Data problems 19 and 20.

Two plain CSV files, unaltered copies of official statistics downloaded on 26 November 2026. The problems send you to the statistical offices first; these copies are here in case a page moves. Cite the source, not this pack.

  • france-hicp-restaurants-hairdressing.csv: Eurostat, Harmonised Index of Consumer Prices, dataset prc_hicp_midx, France, 2015 = 100, monthly from January 1996 to December 2025, categories CP1111 (restaurants, cafes and the like) and CP1211 (hairdressing salons and personal grooming establishments). Eurostat authorises reuse of its data provided the source is acknowledged.
  • finland-cpi-hairdressing.csv: Statistics Finland, Consumer Price Index (2005 = 100), archive table 11xf, monthly from January 2005 to December 2025, commodities 12111, 12112 and 12113. Statistics Finland's open data are covered by the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence.
  • README.txt (every column)
  • The sources: Eurostat, prc_hicp_midx and Statistics Finland, table 11xf
