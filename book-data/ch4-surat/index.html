Surat emissions market data pack

Public Economics for Developing Countries, Chapter 4, Evidence Spotlight "A market for smoke in Surat".

One plain CSV file cut from the replication data of Greenstone, Pande, Ryan and Sudarshan, "Can Pollution Markets Work in Developing Countries? Experimental Evidence from India," Quarterly Journal of Economics 140(2), 2025 (Harvard Dataverse, doi:10.7910/DVN/PDFDKR; CC BY 4.0). It holds the plant-month emissions the chapter's Rebuild-it steps use. Stata, R, Python and Excel all read it. Cite the article, not this pack.

  • ch4-surat-data.zip (the CSV file and the README)
  • surat-plant-month.csv (one row per plant and month, 7,008 rows)
  • README.txt (every column, and the chapter's check values)
